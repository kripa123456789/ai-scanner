1. Privacy Principles & Overview

KSGameplays is committed to protecting your privacy. Guide For Off The Road OTR is built as an offline-first reference utility designed solely to provide game walkthroughs, vehicle specifications, map guides, collectible locations, and tips for players. Our core privacy principles are:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, provide an email address, or create a user profile to use the App.
  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell personal identifiers such as your name, email, phone number, address, or location.
  • No Advertising SDKs: The App contains no advertising networks (no Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or Facebook Audience Network) and does not collect or use the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID).
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not incorporate third-party analytics trackers, session recorders, or behavioral profiling tools (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Adjust, or AppsFlyer).
  • Offline Reference: All vehicle catalogs, maps, cards, challenges, and guide content are bundled directly into the local application package.

2. Data Handling & Storage Architecture

Because Guide For Off The Road OTR is an informational reference utility, all features run locally on your device:

  • Private Local Storage: The App may store basic, non-personal interface states (such as last-viewed pages, scroll positions, or local user bookmarks) inside standard Android private storage (SharedPreferences / application cache).
  • No External Server Transmission: No personal or gameplay data is transmitted to external developer servers, cloud databases, or third-party storage providers.

3. Android Permissions Declared in the App

To ensure total transparency, the following technical permissions declared in the application package are detailed below along with their specific technical scope:

  • android.permission.INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: These permissions are standard boilerplate dependencies included by the application framework runtime (React Native / AppGyver). The guide's content and imagery are bundled locally within the application; the App does not transmit your personal data or user telemetry across external networks.
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: These legacy permissions were declared by the framework's internal FileProvider for local caching of bundled guide assets on older Android releases. The App does not inspect, access, read, or upload your personal photos, media files, documents, or external downloads.
  • android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE: This legacy entry was bundled by default in older cross-platform framework templates. The App does not access your phone call status, call history, phone number, cellular subscriber identity (IMSI), or hardware IMEI.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Allows the device screen to remain illuminated while a player is reading walkthroughs, studying maps, or following vehicle upgrade guides without the display timing out.
  • com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE: Standard Google Play service permission used by the operating system to verify authentic installation through Google Play.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Guide For Off The Road OTR is completely ad-free and tracker-free:

  • We do not partner with advertising brokers, data brokers, or behavioral analytics providers.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user information or device identifiers.
  • We do not collect or declare the Google Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).

5. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Under Google Play Developer Policies, developers must define a clear retention and deletion policy:

  • Retention: Since KSGameplays does not collect, transmit, or store any personal user data on external servers, we retain zero personal user records.
  • Deletion Mechanism: Any local preference data or cached assets stored by the App on your device can be permanently erased at any time by clearing application data via:
    Android Settings > Apps > Guide For Off The Road OTR > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by completely uninstalling the application from your device.

6. Security of User Data

We take data security seriously. Because Guide For Off The Road OTR operates locally on your Android device and does not collect, serialize, or transmit personal data over public networks, the risk of external data exposure or interception is mitigated by architectural design. All internal app assets and configuration settings are guarded within Android’s isolated application sandbox.
