Companion for OTR2 — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026
Last Updated: November 4, 2026
Developer: KS Gameplay • Contact Email: aiscanner.support@gmail.com

Disclaimer & Fan Notice: Companion for OTR2 (“the App”) is an unofficial, fan-made utility for the Off The Road 2 mobile game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Dogbyte Games. All game names, vehicle designs, and trademarks are the intellectual property of their respective owners.

1. Privacy Summary

  • No User Account: No sign-up, login, email registration, or social profile is required to use any feature of the App.
  • Local-First Architecture: All saved vehicle setups, customized tuning profiles, ECU inventories, and preference choices are stored exclusively on your device.
  • 100% Ad-Free (Version 1.0): The App contains no advertising SDKs, no ad trackers, and no advertising identifiers.
  • Zero Cloud Photo Storage: The Screenshot Analyzer reads game pictures locally on your device. Your screenshots or photos are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The App does not request camera, GPS location, contacts, microphone, or external storage access.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following non-personal game reference data locally in private application storage (Android SharedPreferences):

  • Vehicles you mark as owned or favorite;
  • Saved upgrade builds and recorded vehicle tuning setups;
  • Your custom ECU module inventory;
  • Your theme choice (Light, Dark, or System mode);
  • AI Companion local chat history (the last 100 interaction records, stored on-device only).

This data never leaves your device through the App. You can erase this data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > OTR2 Companion > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Screenshot Analyzer & Photos

The Screenshot Analyzer relies on Android’s modern system Photo Picker:

  • The App only accesses the individual screenshot you explicitly pick. It does not request broad photo library or gallery permissions.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and upgrade bar detection are executed directly on your device using bundled Google ML Kit models.
  • Extracted numbers and vehicle stats are processed entirely in memory. Temporary cache images created during image selection are automatically deleted immediately after processing.

4. Third-Party Services (Google ML Kit)

The App uses Google ML Kit text recognition for on-device OCR. As documented in the Google ML Kit Android Data Disclosure, Google may transmit limited diagnostic and performance telemetry to Google servers over encrypted HTTPS:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version;
  • App package name and version;
  • ML Kit feature configuration, processing performance metrics, and error codes.

Your pictures, extracted text, and personal identifiers are never included in this telemetry.

5. AI Companion

The built-in AI Companion provides answers using an on-device, deterministic game guide, vehicle catalog, and curated FAQ database. It does not transmit questions or chat logs to any external generative AI server or third-party cloud. You can clear your question history at any time using the “Clear” button inside the chat interface.

6. No Advertisements

Companion for OTR2 (Version 1.0) is completely ad-free. The App contains no advertising SDKs, no ad trackers, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. We do not collect or sell user data.

7. Permissions Declared

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Declared solely for Google ML Kit to transmit basic technical diagnostic telemetry (as described in Section 4). The App’s internal logic performs no external network calls.
  • The App does not request, declare, or use the Advertising ID (AD_ID).
  • The App requests no runtime dangerous permissions (no camera, microphone, exact location, or contact permissions).

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed as a utility and guide for general gaming audiences aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
